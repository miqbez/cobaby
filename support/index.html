Cobaby Support

Need a hand with Coby? Here are answers to common questions.

Still stuck? Email miqbez@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

How do I add Coby to my Home Screen?

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit in the top corner, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for Cobaby, pick a size and tap Add Widget.

How do I add Coby to my Lock Screen?

  1. Touch and hold your Lock Screen, then tap Customize.
  2. Choose Lock Screen, then tap the widget area below the time.
  3. Pick Cobaby.

How do I give Coby a pat?

Tap Coby in the app or on any widget. Coby lights up with hearts for a few seconds.

Can I change when Coby sleeps and eats?

Yes. Open Cobaby, go to Settings, and set Coby's bedtime, wake-up time, breakfast and dinner.

Can I stop a pose from showing up?

Yes. Open the Gallery, tap the pose, and turn off "Show this pose". Coby needs at least one everyday pose left on.

My widget isn't updating.

Widgets refresh on a schedule set by iOS, so Coby can take a few minutes to change. Giving Coby a pat in the app refreshes every widget right away. If one still looks stuck, remove it and add it again.
