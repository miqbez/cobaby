Cobaby Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Cobaby is an app featuring Coby, a pixel-art cat who lives on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Cobaby.

The short version

Cobaby does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Everything the app needs stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. Cobaby has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking or SDKs. The app does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

To work, Cobaby saves a few settings on your device only:

  • Coby's bedtime, wake-up time, and mealtimes
  • Which poses you have turned off in the Gallery
  • How many times you have patted Coby, and when you last did

This information is shared only between the Cobaby app and its widgets on the same device. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Children's privacy

Because Cobaby collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
